Why Choose Our School?

Competitive Benefits & Perks:

    • Certified Staff may receive $675/month towards health insurance
    • Free housing for the first year and just $200/month after that
    • Utilities (except internet) paid
    • Opportunities for coaching & advising positions

A Supportive and Experienced Team:

    • Some of our teachers have over 20 years of experience, and many serve as mentors to new educators.
    • We offer a tight-knit, collaborative environment where everyone pitches in to help.

A Positive Learning Environment:

    • Small class sizes allow for personalized learning and individual attention.
    • Our students are hardworking and respectful, with many on the Honor Roll and Superintendent’s Honor Roll.
    • We celebrate all of our students and their achievements, whether it’s on the court, on the stage, in the field, or in the classroom.

A 4-Day School Week:

    • Enjoy a 4-day school week, giving you a long weekend every week to recharge and spend time with family or pursue personal interests.

Why Choose Our Town?

A Safe, Family-Friendly Community:

  • We are a safe, welcoming town where everyone knows everyone.

  • Low crime rates and peaceful living make this the perfect place to raise a family.

A Dedicated Farming & Ranching Community:

  • Our town is built on hard work, strong values, and a deep-rooted dedication to farming and ranching.

  • We take pride in our agricultural heritage and the hardworking people who make our community thrive.

A Low-Cost, Convenient Lifestyle:

  • The low cost of living ensures you can enjoy a comfortable life without the high expenses found in larger cities.

  • Essential amenities like stores, restaurants, churches, and post office are all right here.

  • We want people to come and stay—our town is always ready to welcome new residents with open arms!

Recreational Opportunities & Events:

  • Our town is known for its outdoor activities, including hunting, making it an ideal destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

  • The Big Flat Community hosts a variety of annual events that bring the community together, including the Vendor Show, Big Flat Fun Day – Car Show & Fly In, and 4th of July Parade & BBQ.

  • Locals stay busy and connected with a variety of fun activities, from painting classes and cornhole tournaments to bingo nights and community gatherings. There’s always something happening to bring friends and neighbors together!

Location Perks:

  • We’re located just 12 miles from the Canadian border, giving you access to cross-border experiences and opportunities.

  • Outdoor enthusiasts will love our proximity to Nelson Reservoir and Fresno Reservoir, perfect for boating, fishing, and water sports. The Milk River also offers excellent fishing opportunities.

  • Relax and unwind at Sleeping Buffalo Hot Springs, a great spot to soak year-round.

  • Need a shopping trip? Havre, MT is just a short drive away for additional shopping, dining, and entertainment options.

  • Camping & Outdoor Adventures: Enjoy scenic camping and recreation at Missouri River Breaks, the Little Rocky Mountains, and the Bears Paw Mountains, offering breathtaking landscapes and outdoor activities for nature lovers.
