Employment Opportunities

Explore the career opportunities currently available at Miles Community College (MCC). MCC is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to maintaining an environment of respect and acceptance, one that recognizes the inherent worth and dignity of every person who enters our doors. We believe that a rich diversity of people and the many points of view they bring to campus serve to enhance the quality of the educational experience at MCC. Together, our community strives to create and maintain working and learning environments that are inclusive, equitable and welcoming.

Dining Services Assistant: Full Description (PDF)
Miles Community College is currently accepting applications for a full-time Dining Services Assistant.
About this Role
This is a full-time, 9-month contract with benefits available for 12 months.  This position is responsible for assisting the Dining Services Director in all aspects of the food service operations to promote customer satisfaction and to maximize profitability.  The typical hours are Monday-Friday 5:30am-1:30pm, 7:00am-3:00pm, or 12:00noon-8:00pm depending upon availability. Opportunities for summer employment may be available.  Employees should complete ServSafe training within the first six months of employment.
Key Responsibilities
    • Ensure food is professionally prepared and served
    • Provide exceptional customer service
    • Coordinate catering services
    • This individual will help with breakfast and lunch service and assist with morning operations. Summer employment opportunities may be available.

Qualifications:
Required
    • High School Diploma
    • 2 – 3 years of appropriate work experience
Preferred
    • Effective leadership and organizational skills
    • Must be able to work independently and collaboratively to meet food service and campus wide objectives.

Application Process:
To apply for this position, please send a cover letter, résumé, list of three references, and a completed MCC Employment Application and Background Check Information Form to Jodi Stone, Human Resources Coordinator, Miles Community College, 2715 Dickinson Street, Miles City MT 59301

Learning Center Instructor: Full Description (PDF)
Miles Community College is currently looking for an experienced and motivated Learning Center Instructor who is able to support the academic needs of college students and teach or instruct out-of-school youth and adults in preparatory classes for the High School Equivalency Test, English as a Second Language, and basic college and career readiness skills.
About this Role
This is a full-time, academic year position with a start date of August 15 and end date of May 15, with three additional summer work days required to support college student orientation. The contract is payable over 12 months with a 12-month competitive benefits package. This position will instruct adult education/high school equivalency prep classes and tutor and provide general academic and study skill support to college students in a variety of subjects. Teaching and/or tutoring may take place in a traditional classroom or via remote delivery.
 
Key Responsibilities
    • Instruct adult students in basic skill development, High School Equivalency preparatory content, digital literacy, or English as a Second Language.
    • Provide tutoring to adult education and college students as needed, and train and supervise college student peer tutors.
    • Advise adult learners and MCC college students on career and college readiness skills and options.
    • Provide ad hoc academic advising and advise a pool of college students, as needed.
    • Assist college students in navigating and provide student support for various campus programs and educational platforms.
    • Support the Director of Student Success and Retention in providing services and monitoring the progress of college students registered with Disability Support Services, as well as college students in academic warning or suspension status.

Qualifications:
    • Bachelor’s degree in education or closely related field required.
Preferred:
    • Active Montana teaching certificate.

Library Associate: Full Description (PDF)
Miles Community College is seeking a dynamic, customer-focused, and self-motivated Library Associate to support the daily operations of the College Library. This full-time, 10-month position provides direct access to library resources, services, technology, and instructional support for students, employees, and community patrons.
 
The Library Associate is responsible for circulation, cataloging, interlibrary loan, patron account management, fee processing, stacks maintenance, library programming, displays, and assisting patrons with library hardware and software. This position also supports library instruction, creates promotional and instructional materials, coordinates library-based cultural events, and supervises student workers.
 
The successful candidate will possess excellent customer service and communication skills, strong organizational abilities, sound
judgment and the ability to prioritize multiple responsibilities while working independently in a fast-paced environment with frequent interruptions. The position requires accuracy, confidentiality, attention to detail, and a commitment to providing an inclusive and welcoming learning environment for all library users.
 
Work Schedule: Monday–Friday, 9:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. This is a full-time, 10-month classified staff position.

Qualifications:
    • Minimum of an associate degree required; bachelor's degree preferred.
    • Library experience preferred.
    • Experience providing exceptional customer service in a public-facing environment.
    • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and the ability to learn and utilize library systems, databases, software, and technology.
    • Ability to communicate effectively both orally and in writing.
    • Strong organizational, problem-solving, and time management skills.
    • Ability to work independently and collaboratively as part of a team.
    • Ability to maintain confidentiality and exercise sound judgment.
    • Ability to lift and move up to 30 pounds of library materials and push library carts weighing up to 100 pounds.

Application Process:
To apply for this position, please send a cover letter, résumé, list of three references, unofficial college transcripts, and a completed MCC Employment Application and Background Check Information Form to Jodi Stone, Human Resources, Miles Community College, 2715 Dickinson Street, Miles City MT 59301

Director of Institutional Advancement: Full Description (PDF)
Miles Community College is seeking an innovative, relationship-focused, and strategic Director of Institutional Advancement to lead the College's fundraising, grant development, alumni engagement, and institutional communications efforts.
 
About this Role
The Director of Institutional Advancement provides strategic leadership for private fundraising, public and private grant development, alumni relations, Foundation support, and institutional advancement communications. This position works closely with the President, the Miles Community College Foundation, community partners, donors, and campus leadership to secure resources that advance the College's mission and support student success.
 
Key Responsibilities
    • Lead fundraising initiatives to support scholarships, academic programs, capital projects, and other institutional priorities.
    • Identify, develop, write, and manage public and private grant opportunities while ensuring compliance with reporting requirements.
    • Serve as the primary liaison to the Miles Community College Foundation and support Foundation Board activities.
    • Develop and implement alumni engagement initiatives that strengthen relationships and expand philanthropic support.
    • Oversee institutional advancement communications, marketing, and public relations activities that support advancement efforts.
    • Manage scholarship programs, donor records, and advancement reporting systems.
    • Represent the College with community organizations, donors, businesses, government agencies, and other external partners.

Qualifications:
    • Bachelor's degree from a regionally accredited institution in communications, marketing, business, nonprofit management, public administration, or a related field.
    • Demonstrated experience in fundraising, grant writing, development, marketing, communications, or related advancement activities.
    • Excellent written and verbal communication, organizational, project management, and relationship-building skills.
    • Strong proficiency with Microsoft Office products and the ability to manage multiple priorities while maintaining a high level of confidentiality.
    • Valid driver's license and the ability to travel and work occasional evenings and weekends.
Preferred:
    • Experience working with nonprofit organizations, higher education, or community foundations.
    • Experience with donor management, grant management, or customer relationship management (CRM) systems.
    • Knowledge of philanthropic best practices, donor stewardship, planned giving, and alumni engagement.

Application Process:

Adjunct Faculty Opportunities:
Miles Community College is seeking qualified, student-centered adjunct faculty members to support our continued growth and commitment to providing high-quality educational opportunities across eastern Montana.
As enrollment continues to grow and new courses emerge, opportunities may become available across a wide range of subject areas. Courses may be offered on campus, online, and hybrid formats. We encourage qualified professionals from all disciplines to express their interest.
We welcome applications from individuals interested in teaching in a variety of disciplines, with current and anticipated needs including:
  • Anatomy & Physiology
  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Composition and English
  • Microbiology
  • Psychology
  • Additional academic, career and technical education, and workforce development disciplines
At Miles Community College, we are committed to fostering a culture of collaboration, mutual respect, continuous improvement, and student success. Adjunct instructors are important members of our academic community and are supported by colleagues who are committed to creating a welcoming, positive, and engaging environment for both employees and students. We believe great ideas come from all members of our college community, and we strive to create an environment where faculty feel valued, supported, and empowered to make a meaningful impact on students' lives.

Qualifications:
Master's degree in the teaching discipline or a master's degree with at least 9 graduate semester credits in the teaching discipline are generally required. Relevant professional experience and prior teaching experience are preferred but not required.

Application Process:
Interested candidates should submit a cover letter, résumé or curriculum vitae, unofficial transcripts, and contact information for three professional references.
