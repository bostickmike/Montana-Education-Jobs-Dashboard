Current job opportunities: 

Thank you for taking the time to view our available positions, we are excited that you are considering employment with Dayton School!

Teachers: please fill out our Certified Teacher Application and send your resume to U.W.S. – Dayton School #33. attn: RaSena Christopher, P.O. Box 195 Dayton, MT 59914.

Paraprofessionals: Duties may include copying, supervising recesses, helping serve lunch, working with students one-on-one, and working with the Staff throughout the school day schedule. Must enjoy working with children, be reliable, and organized. Please fill out the classified employment application and send your resume to U.W.S. – Dayton School District #33 attn: RaSena Christopher, P.O. Box 195 Dayton, MT 59914.  Paraprofessional Job Description

Substitute Teachers: Thank you for your interest in substitute teaching at Dayton School. As a substitute, you are an important part of our team. Many of our substitutes live in our wonderful district. Dayton school is proud to be part of a community that wholeheartedly supports us. Retired teachers are encouraged to apply. Substitutes work oncall, 7:30 AM – 4:30 PM, and must pass a criminal background check. Please fill out the classified employment application and send your resume to U.W.S. – Dayton School District #33 attn: RaSena Christopher, P.O. Box 195 Dayton, MT 59914.

For questions regarding position qualifications or application procedures, contact RaSena Christopher: School 849-5484, or rchristopher@daytonschool.net
