Teaching & School Staff Jobs in Winnett, Montana

Come teach at Winnett Public Schools! A K-12 School in Central Montana, we are at the heart of a tight-knit, small town community. Our students excel, student to teacher ratios are ideal, and we offer multiple benefits. Winnett is also known as a wonderful place to recreate, we are along the Lewis & Clark cycling trail and a fishing and hunting mecca. Open teaching positions and other jobs available are below.

Benefits & Local Living:

  • 4 day work week

  • Free lunch and breakfast on school days.

  • Spots available in local daycare.

  • Early Learning program for 4-5 year olds.

  • Local after-school programs available.

  • Affordable housing available in town with more being built.

  • Supplemental Income of $7950/year for a full time employees in lieu of insurance.

  • Use of school weight room and gym facilities.
