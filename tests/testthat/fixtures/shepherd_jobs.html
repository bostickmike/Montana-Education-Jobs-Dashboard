Overview

 We Are Shepherd: A safe, respectful, and positive community where our high expectations and emphasis on personal growth support all students in being successful, lifelong learners.

 
Administrator Positions:

 

Certified Teaching Positions:
  • Elementary Teacher
  • Middle School Art Teacher
  • Middle School Business/Technology Teacher
  • Middle School Teacher
  • Special Education Teacher
 

For all open Certified Positions listed above, please fill out a Shepherd Certified application. With your application, please provide a letter of interest, your resume, a copy of your transcripts, three (3) letters of recommendation, and a Montana Certified Teacher's License (or proof that it is in process). You can email your completed application and additional documents to Mrs. Mckenna Dennison at m-dennison@shepherd.k12.mt.us   or mail Mckenna Dennison attention at Shepherd School, PO Box 8, Shepherd, MT 59079. 

Shepherd Schools offers medical, dental and vision as well as supplemental insurance. The district contributes up to $780 per month towards the plan/plans of your choosing. 

Classified Positions:

  • High School Paraprofessional
  • Middle School Paraprofessional
  • Substitute Nurse
  • Kitchen Staff

Coaching Positions:

  • Head High School Cheer Coach
  • Assistant High School Wrestling Coach
  • Assistant High School Wrestling Coach
  • Assistant High School Girls' Basketball Coach
  • Assistant High School Boys Basketball Coach
  • 5th & 6th Grade Girls Basketball Coach
  • Assistant Middle School Track & Field Coach
  • Head High School Baseball Coach
  • Assistant High School Baseball Coach

 

  • Substitute Bus Drivers Please contact Bobby Trotter at 406-373-5331 or r-trotter@shepherd.k12.mt.us or go to the Transportation tab under District Office to receive more information. The starting pay for a bus driver will depend on experience and substitute bus driver's pay is $18.90/hour.
  • Substitute Teachers, Aides/Paraprofessionals, kitchen helpers, etc. We can always use substitutes, and you don't have to have any special training to be a substitute. (Certified teachers are always welcome as well).  You can choose to sub for teachers, teachers' aides, secretaries, etc.
  • Substitute Daycare Aides Please contact Cindy Todd for more information, c-todd@shepherd.k12.mt.us for more information.

To apply for an open Classified Position listed above, please fill out a Shepherd Classified application. You can email your completed application to Mckenna Dennison at m-dennison@shepherd.k12.mt.us , or mail to the address provided above.

Shepherd School District #37 does not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, gender, age, national origin, disability or marital status.
