HFS is actively seeking individuals for TWO open positions - Maintenance/Custodian Supervisor and Special Education Para Professional

1. Maintenance/Custodial Supervisor

Helena Flats School is looking for a Maintenance/Custodial Supervisor. Hourly wage DOE beginning at $23.00/hour plus benefits package.

If interested, please send the completed Maintenance Application (Maintenance Application - Classified) to: 

Andy Maheras, Helena Flats School Superintendent, 1000 Helena Flats Rd., Kalispell, MT 59901 or supt@helenaflats.org

Helena Flats School is an Equal Opportunity Employer

Leadership & Maintenance Oversight:

Supervision: Plans, schedules, and directs the work of assigned personnel; organizes, leads, and inspects work to ensure industry standards are met.

Coordination: Works with school administrators to schedule repairs for buildings, equipment, and District property.

Budget & Records: Assists in maintenance budget preparation and maintains accurate records of time, resources, and costs.

Safety & Security: Ensures buildings are secured at the end of shifts and reports any unruly activity, loitering, or vandalism.

Facility Care & Manual Labor:

Cleaning: Performs comprehensive cleaning, including sweeping, mopping, waxing floors, vacuuming carpets, and washing windows/walls.

Sanitation: Cleans and disinfects restrooms, replenishes supplies, and empties trash receptacles.

Repairs: Performs routine non-technical maintenance using hand tools and may prepare/paint interior and exterior surfaces.

Grounds & Events: Arranges furniture for special activities.

Desired Minimum Qualifications

High school diploma or equivalent supplemented by advanced training in maintenance and construction;

Valid Montana driver’s license.

Knowledge of methods, materials, tools, and equipment, trade practices and techniques, and quality standards applicable to maintenance and repair of buildings and grounds.

Knowledge of building maintenance practices, materials, and laws or codes affecting the construction and repair of school buildings.

Knowledge of cost estimation and controls.

 Knowledge of efficient supply operations and proper record keeping.

Knowledge of general principles of supervision and training.

Knowledge of methods, techniques, materials, and equipment used in maintenance, repair, and construction of school facilities and buildings.

 Knowledge of safe working methods and procedures.

Organize, lead, inspect, and monitor work of maintenance personnel.

Establish priorities and make sound operational decisions.

Establish and maintain effective and cooperative relationships with others.

Communicate effectively orally and in writing.

2. Special Education Paraprofessional

Helena Flats School is looking for a Special Education Paraprofessional to serve as a one on one support for special needs students. Our top priority is the safety and well-being of our students. Hourly wage beginning at $15.00/hour plus benefits package.

If interested, please send the completed Classified Application (Job Application - Classified) to: 

Andy Maheras, Helena Flats School Superintendent, 1000 Helena Flats Rd., Kalispell, MT 59901 or supt@helenaflats.org

Helena Flats School is an Equal Opportunity Employer

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:

Instructional Support: Adapts and presents modified learning materials; assists individual students or small groups to reinforce IEP goals and classroom instruction.

Physical & Communication Support: Assists wheelchair-bound and nonverbal students with mobility, transfers, positioning, personal care, and Augmentative/Alternative Communication (AAC) devices or visual tools.

Behavior & Safety: Implements Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports (PBIS), maintains safety protocols, and monitors student behavior.

Data & Clerical: Tracks progress data, maintains behavioral logs, prepares adapted coursework, and manages classroom inventory/equipment.

Environment: Maintains a safe, accessible, sensory-appropriate, and orderly learning environment.

Performs other related duties as assigned.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:

Education: Two years of higher education study, an associate degree (or higher), or passing score on a state/local academic assessment.

Knowledge: Child development, exceptionalities, special education inclusion, and specialized physical/behavioral support strategies.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

Skills: Operating AAC devices/assistive tech; executing safe physical transfer/positioning techniques; routine data collection and clerical work.

Abilities: Interpret nonverbal cues; execute IEP accommodations; maintain strict confidentiality; build positive relationships; remain calm in crisis situations.

EQUIPMENT USED: AAC systems, tablets, computers, copiers, and adaptive equipment (wheelchairs, lifts, positioning aids).

WORK ENVIRONMENT: Classrooms, playgrounds, and school grounds. Noise levels range from moderate to occasionally high.

PHYSICAL DEMANDS: Frequent sitting, standing, walking, bending, and kneeling. Must hear in quiet and noisy environments. Frequently required to safely lift, transfer, or position students weighing up to 50 lbs (or more with assistance/lifts).

MENTAL & MOTOR DEMANDS: Requires high flexibility, task-switching, continuous attentiveness, active monitoring of nonverbal cues, and de-escalation skills under time constraints.

Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform essential functions.

Why Join Helena Flats School District?

At Helena Flats, we are committed to providing a supportive and enriching educational environment for our students and staff. As a Paraprofessional, you will have the opportunity to make a significant impact on the lives of young learners, helping to shape their future success.
