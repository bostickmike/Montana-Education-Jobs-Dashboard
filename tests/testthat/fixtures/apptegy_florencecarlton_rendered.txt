OPEN Certified Positions

HIGH SCHOOL COUNSELOR                                               

 Position Type: Certified, Exempt

Schedule: Adopted School Calendar + 7 PIR (Professional Instructional Related) Days

Evaluation: As prescribed by the CBA

Supervisor: Building Principal

 Position Summary

 

The General Education Counselor supports the academic, career, social-emotional, and behavioral development of students across all grade levels. Using clinical assessments, evidence-based interventions, and collaborative strategies, the counselor promotes student well-being, college and career readiness, and school-wide wellness practices. This role includes proactive communication with families, teachers, and community partners and utilizes Infinite Campus to maintain accurate documentation and student support records.

 

 Key Responsibilities (specific areas may relate to specific grade levels):

 

·         Provide direct counseling services for academic planning, emotional regulation, behavioral support, and conflict resolution

·         Conduct clinical assessments to identify individual student needs and develop care strategies

·         Support college readiness programming including transcript reviews, post-secondary planning, financial aid awareness, and scholarship coordination

·         Deliver career advising through interest inventories, career exploration, dual credit guidance, and workplace readiness preparation

·         Train staff and students in school wellness initiatives, including mental health awareness, stress management, and social-emotional learning

·         Implement and support behavioral interventions aligned with Positive Behavioral Interventions and Supports (PBIS), restorative practices, or MTSS

·         Coordinate referrals and collaborate with external agencies for extended student services

·         Participate in parent-teacher conferences, open house events, and family engagement efforts

·         Monitor crisis response and provide support for emergency or safety-related incidents

·         Participate in 7 PIR days focused on professional development, case management, student care planning, and collaboration

·         Maintain confidential student records using Infinite Campus, including counseling notes, assessments, and behavior documentation

·         Collaborate on 504 Plans, academic interventions, and student success strategies with multidisciplinary teams

·         Support school culture by contributing to prevention programs, peer mentoring, and inclusivity initiatives

·         Other duties as assigned




Qualifications

 

·         Master’s degree in School Counseling or related field required

·         Valid Montana license with School Counselor endorsement

·         Experience in K–12 student support services, college/career advising, or wellness program facilitation preferred

·         Knowledge of IDEA, Section 504, FERPA, and state education policies

·         Proficiency in Infinite Campus and digital platforms used for counseling and student support

·         Excellent interpersonal, communication, and organizational skills

·         Ability to work collaboratively and respond flexibly to student needs

·         Commitment to equity, cultural responsiveness, and whole-child support

 

Work Environment

 

·         School campuses and counseling offices serving students across grade levels

·         Regular interaction with students, families, faculty, administrators, and community partners

·         May require flexible scheduling for student care meetings, crisis response, or PIR activities

OPEN Classified Positions

SEEKING TWO (2) EVENING SHIFT SCHOOL CUSTODIANS   

AND ONE (1) MID SHIFT SCHOOL CUSTODIAN                                 

Reports to: Facilities Director

Employment Type: Full-Time, Year-Round

Evaluation: Performance evaluated annually

Pay Range (DOE): $16.80 - $19.50

 Position Summary:

The General Custodian ensures a safe, clean, and welcoming environment for students, staff, and visitors across school facilities. This position plays a vital role in maintaining the standards of cleanliness, sanitation, and functionality in classrooms, offices, common areas, and exterior grounds.

 Key Responsibilities

·         Perform routine cleaning duties: sweeping, mopping, vacuuming, dusting, and restroom sanitation

·         Ensure building entrances, hallways, and classrooms are clean and orderly

·         Monitor and replenish supplies (soap, paper towels, toilet tissue, etc.)

·         Dispose of trash and recycling according to school policies

·         Assist with setting up and breaking down rooms for events, meetings, or activities

·         Respond promptly to custodial-related work orders or facility needs

·         Report maintenance issues or safety hazards to the Facilities Director

·         Support seasonal tasks such as snow removal, lawn care, or minor landscaping

·         Secure buildings by locking doors and windows at designated times

·         Follow all health, safety, and district regulations

·         General duties as assigned

 Qualifications & Requirements

·         High school diploma or equivalent

·         Prior custodial experience preferred, but not required

·         Ability to operate cleaning equipment and tools safely and effectively

·         Basic understanding of cleaning chemicals and safe handling practices

·         Ability to lift and move up to 50 pounds and perform physical tasks on a regular basis

·         Strong attention to detail and reliability

·         Good communication and teamwork skills

 Work Schedule & Compensation

·         Full-time position (40 hours/week), with occasional evenings or weekends for school events

·         Year-round employment

·         Compensation according to district custodial pay scale; includes benefits as applicable

OPEN Coaching Positions

SEEKING A MIDDLE SCHOOL GIRLS BASKETBALL COACH FOR THE 2026-27 SCHOOL YEAR

ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS:

 Coaches participants in the skills necessary for the sport/activity assigned.

 Establishes a schedule of practices.

 Maintains discipline and sportsmanlike conduct of all participants and enforces the policies of the District uniformly when disciplining participants

 In addition to those established by MHSA and Board policy, establishes performance criteria for eligibility. 

 Responsible for the inventory of equipment and supplies.

 Responsible for making recommendations to the administration for equipment and supplies needed for the ensuing season.

 Works with the athletic director in scheduling intramural and interscholastic contests.

 In team activities, teaches/coaches the importance of team work.

 Only minimum duties are listed.  Other functions may be required as given or assigned.

 

DESIRED MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS:

 ·         Meets MHSA requirements (if applicable).

·         Holds a currently valid American Red Cross First Aid card (for those coaching athletics).

·         Ability to read and interpret documents such as safety rules, operating and maintenance instructions, and procedure manuals.

·         Ability to write routine reports and correspondence.

·         Ability to effectively communicate, orally and in writing, with students, parents, staff, and the community in general.

·         Ability to handle stressful situations.

·         Ability to maintain confidentiality of employment and student matters.

·         Ability to perform duties with an awareness of all District requirements and Board policies.

·         Ability to effectively manage time and responsibilities. 

 

 

WORK ENVIRONMENT:  The employee constantly works around others, works with the public, and works inside and outside.  The noise level in the work environment varies, but is frequently loud.  The employee must be able to meet deadlines with severe time constraints. 

 

PHYSICAL DEMANDS: The employee is frequently required to walk and continuously required to stand.  The employee will frequently bend or twist at the neck and waist while performing the duties of this position.  The employee will occasionally be required to reach with hands and arms and stoop, kneel, crouch, or crawl.  The employee continuously uses hand strength to grasp tools.  The employee must occasionally lift and/or move up to 50 pounds.  Specific vision abilities required by this position include close vision, depth perception, and peripheral vision.

 

MENTAL/MOTOR DEMANDS: While performing the duties of this job, the employee rarely performs routine work.  The employee exercises flexibility (ability to shift from one task to another).  Guidance and reinforcement are infrequently available.  The employee frequently works within time constraints and must maintain attentiveness intensity.  The employee is frequently involved in social interactions which require oral and written communication.  Reasoning and exercising judgment are used/required on the job.

 The physical demands, work-environment characteristics, and mental/motor demands described within this job description are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this job.  Reasonable accommodations shall be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.

Equal Employment, Equal Education

Equal Employment Opportunity and Non-Discrimination - The District will provide equal employment opportunities to all persons, regardless of their race, color, religion, creed, national origin, sex, age, ancestry, marital status, military status, citizenship status, use of lawful products while not at work, physical or mental disability, if otherwise able to perform essential functions of a other legally protected categories.  

Equal Education, Nondiscrimination and Sex Equity - The District will make equal educational opportunities available for all students without regard to race, color, national origin, ancestry, sex, ethnicity, language barrier, religious belief, physical or mental handicap or disability, economic or social conditions, or actual or potential marital or parental status.    No student, on the basis of sex, will be denied equal access to programs, activities, services, or benefits or be limited in the exercise of any right, privilege, or advantage, or denied equal access to educational and extracurricular programs and activities.
