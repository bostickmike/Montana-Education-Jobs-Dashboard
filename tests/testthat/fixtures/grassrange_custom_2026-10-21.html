Open Positions

Grass Range School invites individuals to become part of our team by applying for the following available positions.

2026-2027 school year: CDL Bus/Activity Driver and substitutes in all areas.  

Grass Range is in one of the most beautiful areas of Montana. Located in the center of the state, we are 30 miles east of Lewistown and under 100 miles northwest of Billings. Our school has high academic standards as well as an active FFA program, an active 4-H group, and strong sports teams. We offer football, volleyball, basketball, track and cross country through our sports co-op program with Winnett. We are a small, tight knit community that rallies around our school.

In addition to excellent views, central Montana offers its residents great hunting and fishing. We have the Missouri River to the north, Spring Creek to the west, many smaller local creeks and significant public lands for hunting. Keeping with our western roots, in the summer our town hosts a youth rodeo series at the local arena.

Grass Range is a ranching community with cattle production being the primary industry. Cattle raised in central Montana are known for being some of the best quality beef in the country. We have a small general store that carries many interesting Montana made items as well as groceries, hardware and excellent coffee and ice cream.

Our school has recently adopted a 4-day school week. With small class sizes, the school offers teachers the opportunity to be very hands-on with each student. District housing is available for staff. 

These positions are open until filled.
