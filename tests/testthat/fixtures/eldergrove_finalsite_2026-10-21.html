Available Positions

CERTIFIED POSITIONS

Certified Positions

Elder Grove School District is a K-8 district. All of our available Certified Positions are on the OPI website. Please visit:

Montana Jobs for Teachers

Instructions:

  1. Select Elder Grove Elem - 0972 for the district.
  2. In the school box choose All, Elementary, or Middle School.
  3. Then click Search.
  4. If you would like to apply for the position available you can fill in a Certified Application
  5. Then upload your application to the  Application Submission form

We look forward to reviewing your application.

 


 

 

 

 

CLASSIFIED POSITIONS

Paraprofessionals

Hours
Varies depending on position

Job Duties
Include but are not limited to - Performs related work as requested by supervisor and building administrator. J teacher aide, recess duty, and cafeteria aide. Have the ability to perform  moderate physical activity. Ability to maintain effective work relationships.

Experience
One year as  a paraprofessional work preferred but not needed; High School graduate or equivalent required. 

Application Procedure
All applicants are required to fill out a Elder Grove School District Application and pass a background check through fingerprints.

To submit your application fill out the Application Submission form.

Closing Date
Open Until Filled

If you have any questions about employment at Elder Grove School District please fill out Employment Questions form.
