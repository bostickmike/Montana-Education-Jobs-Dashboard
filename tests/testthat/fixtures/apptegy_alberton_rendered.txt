Assistant Cook | Classified Application | 2026–2027 School Year

Alberton School is seeking a dependable and enthusiastic Assistant Cook to join our team!

We are a small, close-knit ELP–12th grade district serving approximately 160 students and 40 staff members, offering a supportive and positive work environment. Our school operates on a four-day school week, providing a great work-life balance.

About the Role:

•Help prepare and serve homemade breakfast and lunch daily
•Assist in providing fresh fruit and vegetable snacks for students
•Work as part of a collaborative and caring team

What We Offer:

•Training provided – no prior experience required
•Pay based on experience
•Health insurance (individual employee coverage)
•Sick and vacation leave accrual, available after probation per Montana state law.

If you enjoy working with students and want to be part of a team that supports healthy, homemade meals, we would love to hear from you!

For more information or to apply, please fill out a classified application.  If you have any questions please contact Amanda Cyr at acyr@alberton.k12.mt.us or (406)722-4413.
