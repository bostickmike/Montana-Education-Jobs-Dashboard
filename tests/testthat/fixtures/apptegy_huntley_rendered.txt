Are you looking for a job that offers professional growth without sacrificing your quality of life? Huntley Project provides the perfect balance. Located just 18 miles east of Billings, Montana—Montana’s largest city—our district offers a peaceful, close-knit community setting while keeping urban amenities within a 20-minute drive. If you love the outdoors, you will enjoy access to hunting, fishing, hiking, skiing, and rich historical sites all within an hour of your front door. Best of all, we operate on a progressive four-day school week, giving you the time to truly enjoy everything Montana has to offer.
