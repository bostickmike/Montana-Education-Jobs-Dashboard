District Employment

Employment opportunities are listed below. You may also find certified positions available at the Montana Office of Public Instruction website. Columbus Public Schools is an equal opportunity employer.

All positions require passing a background check and board approval. All custodial, school nutrition, and maintenance positions will require a pre- employment physical.  Background checks and pre-employment physicals are paid by the district. If interested in any of the following positions, please contact Casey Klasna at 322-5373 or by email at email Casey Klasna.
