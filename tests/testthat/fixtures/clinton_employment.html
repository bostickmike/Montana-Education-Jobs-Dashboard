Substitute Teachers

Clinton School is looking for Substitute Teachers.   This position gives you flexibility to choose your days and hours, with summers off.   You would work with a strong, dedicated staff to support student growth across all areas. 


We need substitutes for many different areas:


-          Classrooms: KU5-8th grade - whole group or small group

-          Small group and individualized instruction for Special Education & Title I

-          Playground, Lunchroom  

-          After School Program 3:15-5:15


**Training is available for these positions.   If you would like to observe prior to filling out an application please let us know.


If you are interested  fill out the Classified Application at the top of this page.


-          Applicants must be able to pass a background check

-          Have a HS Diploma or GED equivalent

-          Daily rate is $95


Submit applications to application@clintoncougars.com


Contact Kathy Schneider for more details at kschneider@clintoncougars.com or 406-825-3113.

Para Position for 26-27

Clinton School is looking for a paraprofessional for the 2026-2027 school year to support

teachers in classrooms. You will work with small groups of students, or individual students,

on all subjects to improve their skills in grades K-8. Paraprofessionals work approximately

35 hours per week, and the work days follow the school calendar year. Please go to

www.clintoncougars.com on the District - Employment tab to fill out a classified application.
