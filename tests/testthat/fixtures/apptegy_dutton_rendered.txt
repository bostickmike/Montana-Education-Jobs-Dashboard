Classified Job Application

Dutton/Brady District Applications are available above, or by contacting the Dutton/Brady High School Office at 406-476-3424. Applicants must submit a district application, three letters of recommendation, a resume, and a copy of their current Montana Licensure.

Employment Opportunities:

Route and Relief School Bus Drivers

CDL with a School Bus Driver Endorsement is required.  We will train if needed.  Please fill out the Classified application on the forms page or call the school - 406-476-3424 - for an application.

Substitute Teachers

We are always welcoming substitute teachers to our Dutton/Brady family. If interested, please fill out an application at our front office or call 406-476-3424 for more information.
