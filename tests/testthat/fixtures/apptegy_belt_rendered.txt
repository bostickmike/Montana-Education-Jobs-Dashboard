Belt School also has classified openings for bus drivers, substitute teachers, and/or games custodians. Starting pay is $14.45 an hour for custodians, no experience needed; $18.20 for bus drivers, CDL is required and district will compensate for training for qualified candidate.

Belt School is looking for individuals who take pride in their work, community, and Belt school to join our support staff.

Please complete a letter of interest, resume, and district application available on our website (www.beltschool.com) or for pick up in the school office.  Incomplete applications will not be considered.

A formal job description is available from the district upon request.  Pre-employment background check is required.
