About Our School

At our school, Everyone Has a Voice. As a small K-12 district serving approximately 140 students on one beautiful campus, we take pride in providing personalized attention and a wide variety of educational programs and extracurricular activities. Our small class sizes mean you'll truly get to know each child and make a lasting impact on their academic journey.

Why Noxon?

Located in the breathtaking Upper Clark Fork Valley of northwest Montana, Noxon offers the perfect blend of small-town charm and outdoor adventure. Our community provides:

Outdoor Paradise: World-class fishing, hunting, skiing, and water sports right at your doorstep

Convenient Location: Just 15 miles from Idaho's panhandle and the stunning Lake Pend Oreille

Recreation Access: Schweitzer Ski Resort in Sandpoint, ID is only one hour away

Urban Amenities: Easy access to Missoula, Kalispell, and Spokane, WA within two hours for shopping, dining, and cultural activities

What We're Looking For

Required: K-8 Teaching Endorsement and Montana Certification eligibility

Essential: Passion for nurturing young learners through their school experience

Bonus: Interest in coaching or leading extracurricular activities

What We Offer

Competitive compensation per our Noxon Education Association (NEA) Bargaining Agreement

Comprehensive benefits including health insurance

School district housing (availability varies)

Additional opportunities for coaching and extracurricular involvement

Supportive community that values education and teachers
