Special Education Full-Time Aide

Posted on 6/18/26 open until filled 

Employment opportunity at Augusta Schools. 

Augusta School District #45 is seeking enthusiastic, driven and passionate applicants for a special education full time aide. This aid position salary will DEPEND ON EXPERIENCE and as a full time employee you have access to benefits including insurance, retirement and more. 

​

The special education paraprofessionals provide critical support to students with special needs and help to make the learning environment more inclusive and supportive.  They add another layer of support to the student under the direction of a certified teacher. SPED Paraprofessionals perform a variety of tasks relating to the physical,  social/emotional, and instructional needs of students in a classroom setting to support academic success and wellbeing of students.

​

To apply please mail or email the following:

  • District application (below) 

  • Resume of experience 

 

Please mail your application to P.O. Box 307 Augusta, MT 59410 or email to kramorini@augusta.k12.mt.us. 

​

Any questions, call Superintendent Kim Ramorini at 406-562-3384.
