It is Fromberg School District's policy to provide and promote an equal employment opportunity to all applicants regardless of race, creed, religion, color, national origin, age,physical or mental disabilties, marital status, or sex.

For any interested employment opportunties, please fill out and complete the appropriate application and send it to Jennifer Hickok, Superintendent.  All applications must be completed in full & submitted with a resume for consideration.  If there are any questions about the employment application or the process, please send all inquiries to Jennifer Hickok, Superintendent.

To make your employment search easier, we have included direct search abilities with the Montana Office of Public Instruction.  Please use the button below.
