EMPLOYMENT

Lolo School District employs approximately 90 individuals to serve its approximately 500 students, its parents, and its community. Approximately half of the employees are in the teacher/administrator area. The other half are support staff employees working in the areas of Food Service, Maintenance/Custodial, Paraprofessional Aides (with a wide range of responsibility areas), Secretaries, and Administrative Specialists.

If you’re interested in seeking employment at Lolo School District, whether full-time, part-time, or as a substitute, please contact Kathy McDonald at 273-6686. Please see the accompanying documents for current openings, Unified Application form, background check forms and other pertinent and required information.
