Human Resources

Swan River School District Human Resources The Human Resource Department serves approximately 25 Swan River School District employees, as well as substitute teachers, and dedicated people who wish to work on behalf of Swan River students. We develop an ongoing relationship with each employee beginning with the recruitment phase of employment and continuing throughout an individual’s career with the district.

It is our vision to provide valuable human resource services in a professional, effective and client friendly manner.

Swan River School District is an Equal Opportunity employer.
