WE ALWAYS NEED SUBSTITUTES!

Are you interested in Subbing at Monforton School District?

If you are interested in becoming a substitute teacher, please email Mrs. Axtman to get the process started. 

laxtman@monfortonschool.org

How does the approval process work?

Mrs. Axtman will work with you to complete your fingerprinting and any required paperwork. Once we receive your background check results from the Department of Justice, you will be notified. 

Upon receipt of the background check, you will need to make an appointment with Ms. Washburn to get new hire paperwork completed at Monforton. Please email her at awashburn@monfortonschool.org to set up an appointment.

We will then be able to put you into our automated sub system Frontline.  

You can work as much or as little as you want.  The system will call you in the morning to let you know of current needs or you can log in to your Frontline account to look at upcoming jobs. It’s as easy as that!  

We appreciate our substitutes so much!!  If you have any questions about the process, call Ms. Washburn at 586-1557.
