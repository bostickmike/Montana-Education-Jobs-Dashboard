Employment Information

Want to be a Warrior?

(Check out our Promotional Video)

With base compensation set at $45,484, we offer a very competitive salary schedule. Credit on the salary schedule will be given up to ten (10) years of contracted teaching experience. Benefits include health, vision and dental insurance. The District pays a large portion of monthly premiums (i.e. single, two party participant with children, and family).  An HSA and HRA is also provided to cover most of the insurance deduction.  District housing may be available for low rent ($350 per month). Finally, we may offer a onetime $1,000 moving stipend for those relocating to our community from outside the general region.
