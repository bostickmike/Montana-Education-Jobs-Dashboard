Fingerprinting & Background Checks

Any employee or volunteer seeking assignment at Lockwood Schools must complete a background investigation. Fingerprints are required with background checks. Lockwood School Board Policy requires that a fingerprint background check be on file in the District Administration Office before any applicant may begin employment or have contact with students in any capacity on behalf of the District until the required background check has been completed, reviewed, and the individual has been cleared by the appropriate District official.

Lockwood School District offers electronic fingerprinting on-site.  To schedule an appointment, contact Lisa via email or by calling 406.252.6022x5.

Applicants who have successfully completed the fingerprinting process within the last three years may qualify for re-dissemination of results. If you believe that you may qualify for this transfer of information, please notify Lisa (above) to initiate the request process.
