MISSION STATEMENT:

Provide meaningful learning opportunities in an environment rich in human, financial, and physical support, to prepare students to be successful when they take their place in post-secondary education, in the job market. and in the community, and to produce lifelong learners with such intellectual abilities that they will be able to continue to learn and to adapt in a changing world.
