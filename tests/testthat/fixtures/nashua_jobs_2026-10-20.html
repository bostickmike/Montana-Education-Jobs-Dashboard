Employment

Employment Opportunities

 

If you have questions regarding the application process for any of these positions, please contact Marisa Collins at nashuaclerk@nashua.k12.mt.us or 406-746-3411. All applications require two completed fingerprint cards along with the Applicant Rights and Consent to Fingerprint forms. Please see the application options below.

 

Classified Positions: 

Part-Time Custodian

 

Certified Positions:

To apply, please submit a cover letter, resume, three letters of recommendation, college transcripts, teaching certificate, the certified application listed below.

  • K-12 Librarian (full or part-time)
  • Music (full or part-time)
  • Special Education

 

Coaching Positions: 

 

**Coaches and volunteers are required to complete the following prior to the season:** (more information can be provided after hire)

  • Fundamentals of Coaching course
  • Concussion in Sports course
  • Rules Clinic (sport-specific)
  • CPR/First Aid certification
  • Coaching Mental Wellness course

 

Extracurricular Positions: 

Concessions

 

Substitute Positions:

Substitute Teacher

Substitute Bus Driver/Activity Bus Driver
